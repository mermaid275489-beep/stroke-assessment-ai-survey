E056 | 10-Metre Walk Test | Comfortable & fast gait speed over middle 6 m of a 10 m path (2 m accel + 6 m timed + 2 m decel) | time, distance or count | Full
   Continuous output. For each trial, gait speed (m/s) = 6 m divided by the time in seconds between the moment any part of the leading foot crosses the plane of the 2-m mark and the moment any part of the leading foot crosses the plane of the 8-m mark. The two comfortable-speed trials are averaged and the two fast-speed trials are averaged, giving two scores: comfortable gait speed and fast gait speed (m/s). A score of 0 m/s is documented when the patient cannot walk, needs total assistance, or needs assistance for limb swing or for forward propulsion.
E057 | 6-Minute Walk Test | Total distance walked in 6 minutes on a measured course | time, distance or count | Full
   Continuous output: distance in metres (may be rounded to one decimal) = number of completed laps x 12 m + distance of the partial lap completed when the 6 minutes end. If the patient has to sit down before 6 minutes, the test ends and the distance walked up to that point is the score. A score of 0 m is documented if the patient cannot walk, needs total assistance, or needs assistance that moves a limb through swing or affects forward propulsion.
E058 | Timed Up and Go | Stand from chair → walk 3 m → turn → walk back → sit; timed | time, distance or count | Full
   Continuous output: time in seconds, measured from the word 'go' until the patient's buttocks touch the seat (seated again). Shorter times indicate better mobility. Under the Podsiadlo and Richardson procedure one timed trial follows the practice walk-through; some reference guides (for example the AAOP guide) give one practice and two timed trials and record the better (shorter) time. The trial rule used must be stated with the score.
